# Top 10 Hackathon Projects to Build with AI Agents

All ideas below target real pain points and can ship as demo-to-production candidates.

## 1) AgencyOS CRM + Lead Manager (Recommended)
**Problem:** Service agencies lose leads due to scattered inboxes, weak follow-up, and poor handoffs from sales to delivery.
**MVP outcome:** Lead intake, pipeline, automated follow-ups, proposal generator, and client handoff tracker.
**Why hackathon-fit:** Clear workflows, visible UI demo value, measurable ROI.

## 2) Invoice Collection Copilot
**Problem:** SMBs wait too long for payments.
**MVP outcome:** Aging dashboard, reminder automation, promise-to-pay tracking.

## 3) Support Ticket Auto-Triage
**Problem:** Ops teams waste time routing repetitive tickets.
**MVP outcome:** NLP classification, SLA-based routing, suggested responses.

## 4) Meeting-to-Action Engine
**Problem:** Action items from meetings are not tracked.
**MVP outcome:** Auto-summary, owner assignment, due-date reminders, sync to task tools.

## 5) Vendor Contract Renewal Radar
**Problem:** Companies miss renewal windows and overpay.
**MVP outcome:** Contract ingestion, renewal alerts, negotiation checklist.

## 6) Internal Knowledge Assistant (Secure RAG)
**Problem:** Teams lose productivity searching for docs.
**MVP outcome:** Role-aware Q&A over internal docs with source citations.

## 7) Hiring Funnel Manager
**Problem:** Candidate pipeline lacks visibility and follow-up.
**MVP outcome:** Candidate CRM, stage automation, interviewer score aggregation.

## 8) Field Service Dispatch Optimizer
**Problem:** Manual dispatch causes delays.
**MVP outcome:** Priority routing, ETA updates, technician assignment board.

## 9) Compliance Evidence Collector
**Problem:** Audit prep is manual and painful.
**MVP outcome:** Automated evidence pull from Git/Cloud/Identity tools.

## 10) Churn Risk Early Warning System
**Problem:** Customer churn is detected too late.
**MVP outcome:** Health scoring from usage + support signals, renewal risk alerts.

## Selection criteria for your org
- Revenue impact in < 90 days
- Data access availability now
- Integration complexity manageable in hackathon timeline
- Strong internal sponsor and pilot users
