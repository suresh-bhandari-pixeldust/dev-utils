# Pitch Deck Outline (Internal Org Hackathon)

- Current pain with measurable impact (lost leads, delayed follow-up, poor handoff)

- AI agents reduce build cycle from weeks to days
- Existing tools are fragmented and expensive

## Slide 3: Proposed solution
- AgencyOS CRM + Lead Manager
- One workflow from lead capture to delivery handoff

## Slide 4: Live demo flow
- Lead intake → scoring → proposal → won deal → handoff checklist

## Slide 5: Technical approach
- Agent roles, toolchain, and quality gates
- Productionization roadmap

## Slide 6: Business impact
- Faster response time
- Better lead conversion
- Lower operational overhead

## Slide 7: Delivery timeline
- 72-hour hackathon MVP
- 2-4 week production hardening

## Slide 8: Ask
- Pilot team
- Data access approvals
- Sponsor for rollout decision
